# Scale: a shared ruler for space

**Status: proposal, 2026-10-27, for the owner to settle.** One set of units for
the size of maps, the spacing of things, and how far apart the story is.
Distances are in game units (u). The numbers in "Now" are what Seven Flags
uses today.

## The units

| Unit | Size | Why this size |
|---|---|---|
| **Unit (u)** | about 1 metre | Juno is 1.65 u tall; a door is 1.1 u wide and 2 u high |
| **Screen** | about **20 × 18.5 u** on desktop, at Juno's line (15 u wide at the bottom edge, 28 u at the top); a phone in landscape sees about 25 % wider | The camera is fixed (spec §3), so a screen is a real, fixed amount of ground. It's the unit of "something every screen". |
| **Lot** | **8 × 8 u** | One house and its yard |
| **Block** | **32 × 32 u**, which is 4 × 4 lots, about one and a half screens across | The grid we name places on: columns A, B, C…, rows 1, 2, 3… |

**Walking:** Juno walks at 3.1 u/s and runs at 5 u/s.

| Distance | Walking | Running |
|---|---|---|
| One screen (20 u) | 6 s | 4 s |
| One block (32 u) | 10 s | 6 s |
| Seven Flags, end to end (192 u) | 62 s | 38 s |

## Map sizes, in blocks

| Size | Blocks | Units | Use | Now |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Room** | 1 to 3 lots | 8 to 24 u | Inside a house or a shop | The four rooms (11 × 9 to 18 × 11) |
| **Small** | 2 × 2 | 64 × 64 | A side spot or a shade-hold | none yet |
| **Medium** | 4 × 3 | 128 × 96 | A hold or a landmark stop | The White Bowl (132 × 96) |
| **Large** | 6 × 5 | 192 × 160 | A town | Seven Flags (192 × 160) |
| **Road** | 3 × 4, or 2 × 6 for a long leg | 96 × 128, or 64 × 192 | A road leg, long in the direction you travel | The Lake Road (96 × 132) |
| **Road, short** | 3 × 2 | 96 × 64 | A road out, a crossing | The Ramp (88 × 64) |

> **Note:** with this ruler, the owner's earlier "large" (132 × 96) is a
> **Medium**, and the town is **Large**. Snapping the current maps to the grid
> is a small change: the Lake Road to 96 × 128, the White Bowl to 128 × 96,
> and the Ramp to 96 × 64.

## Spacing standards

| Thing | Standard | Now in Seven Flags |
|---|---|---|
| Highway | 13 u: four lanes and a median, on an embankment behind walls | the Thirty-Five |
| Main road | 7 u, plus a 1 u sidewalk each side | Del Mar (7), International, Shiloh, McPherson (6) |
| Side street | 4 u | 21 side streets |
| Lane, path, trail | 2.4 to 3 u | the Old Trail (3) |
| House | 5 to 7 u wide, 4.5 to 6 u deep, 2.5 to 3 u tall | 5 to 7 × 4.4 to 5.4 |
| Big building | 10 to 20 u on a side | Mar Farm 18 × 12; the School 18 × 9; the Big Store 20 × 10; the Warehouse 11 × 15 |
| Between houses | at least 2 u (you can walk between) | yes |
| Front yard | 1.5 to 4 u from the street | about 2 to 3 |
| A plaza | about 1 × ½ block | 32 × 17 |

**How often things come up:**
- **Something to look at:** one per screen.
- **A story place:** 1 to 2 blocks from the next.
- **A hero (landmark):** seen from at least a block away.
- **Lanterns:** about every 22 u along a road at night.
- **People:** at least 3 u apart.
- **Doors you can use:** at least 4 u apart.

## For the owner to settle

1. **The lot and the block.** Are 8 u and 32 u right? Bigger blocks spread
   the town out; smaller ones pack it.
2. **The size names.** Is Medium 4 × 3, Large 6 × 5, Road 3 × 4 right?
3. **Snapping the current maps** to the grid (the note above).
4. **How far apart the story is.** Mar Farm to the Plaza is about 60 u (20 s
   walking). Mar Farm to the on-ramp is about 120 u (40 s).
